## What it does

Under `regulation: "CCPA"` the banner shows a **Do Not Sell** button. This is the dialog it opens: one checkbox and a Save button. Saving with the box ticked rejects every optional category; saving with it unticked accepts them all. After an opt-out, a confirmation shows and the dialog closes by itself after ten seconds; saving without opting out closes it at once.

If the visitor's browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, they start opted out before they see anything.

## Add it

Render it next to the banner whenever your site can be under CCPA. Without it, Do Not Sell does nothing and nothing warns you.

<Framework when="nextjs react">

```tsx check="false"
<CookieBanner />
<CookieOptOut />
```

</Framework>

<Framework when="vue">

```vue check="false"
<CookieBanner />
<CookieOptOut />
```

</Framework>

If you serve GDPR visitors too, render `CookiePreferences` as well. Each dialog appears only under its own regulation.

## Change what it shows

<Framework when="nextjs react">

The wording comes from `i18n` ([Translations](/docs/translations)) and the colours from `theme` ([Theme tokens](/docs/styling/theme-tokens)), both set in `initCookieYes()`. There are no props for them.

</Framework>

<Framework when="vue">

The wording comes from `i18n` ([Translations](/docs/translations)) and the colours from `theme` ([Theme tokens](/docs/styling/theme-tokens)), both set in the config you pass to `app.use(CookieYes, config)`. There are no props for them.

</Framework>

## Restyle it

<Framework when="nextjs react">

It accepts `className` and `style` like any element; they are added to the dialog's own styling. For one part, use its `data-cy-part` in your CSS. The parts are `optout`, `title`, `message`, `confirm` and `close`. For your own markup, use the [OptOut primitives](/docs/headless/opt-out).

</Framework>

<Framework when="vue">

It accepts `class` and `style` like any element; they land on the dialog card and are added to its own styling. To restyle one part, pass `classNames` or `styles`, keyed by part name in camelCase (`root`, `title`, `message`, `checkbox`, `checkboxLabel`, `cancel`, `confirm`, `confirmation`, `close`, `branding`). Or use its `data-cy-part` in your CSS. The parts are `optout`, `title`, `message`, `confirm` and `close`. For your own markup, use the [OptOut primitives](/docs/headless/opt-out).

</Framework>

## Common mistakes

**Do Not Sell does nothing.**
`<CookieOptOut />` is not rendered.

<Framework when="nextjs react">

**The dialog never appears.**
The visitor is not under CCPA. Check `regulation` in `initCookieYes()`.

</Framework>

<Framework when="vue">

**The dialog never appears.**
The visitor is not under CCPA. Check `regulation` in the config you pass to `app.use(CookieYes, config)`.

</Framework>

**Visitors are opted out before touching anything.**
Their browser sends Global Privacy Control, which CookieYes honours by default.
